# Sample datasets

Small copies of public datasets for the Visift app's one-click gallery. They
are cleaned the same way as the [real-world benchmark](../../evaluation/real_world/README.md),
so the app shows what the benchmark measures.

| File | Rows | Source | License |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `penguins.csv` | 344 | [palmerpenguins](https://github.com/allisonhorst/palmerpenguins) | CC0 |
| `titanic.csv` | 1,309 | [Vanderbilt Biostatistics, hbiostat.org/data](https://hbiostat.org/data/) | Free to use with attribution (below) |
| `bike_sharing_day.csv` | 731 | [UCI Bike Sharing](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5W894) | CC BY 4.0 |
| `bank_marketing_sample.csv` | 5,000 | [UCI Bank Marketing](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5K306) | CC BY 4.0 |

The synthetic sales sample is `synthetic_sales.csv` at the repository root
(generated by `scripts/generate_synthetic_sales.py`).

## Changes from the originals

- **Titanic:** only `pclass, survived, sex, age, sibsp, parch, fare, embarked`
  (names, tickets, cabins, destinations, lifeboat and body numbers dropped).
- **Bike Sharing:** UCI `day.csv` without `instant` (a row index) and
  `casual` / `registered` (they sum to the outcome `cnt`).
- **Bank Marketing:** UCI `bank-full.csv` without `duration` (call length is
  only known after the outcome), then a 5,000-row sample stratified on the
  outcome `y` with a fixed seed (`random_state=42`), keeping the original
  11.7% subscription rate. The full 45,211 rows are used by the benchmark.
